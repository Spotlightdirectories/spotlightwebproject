Program Terms & Conditions

1. Commission Structure

  • 20% commission on every successful paid vendor registration
  • 10% commission on renewals
  • ₦30,000 bonus upon reaching 50 paid vendors in a month

2. Refund Policy

  • Refunds allowed within 7 days
  • No refunds after 7 days
  • Vendor consent is captured during onboarding

3. Commission Eligibility

  • Commissions become available after 7 days
  • Refunded transactions cancel commissions

4. Partner Responsibility

  • Partners must communicate refund policy clearly
  • Misrepresentation may lead to suspension

5. Referral Commission

  • 5% commission on directly referred partners
  • No tiers, no complexity — just straightforward earning.
  • Applies only to active partners.
  • Limited to one level and not MLM.

6. Payout Policy

  • Payouts processed between 1st – 5th monthly
  • Minimum payout is ₦10,000

7. Fraud & Abuse

  • No fake or duplicate vendors
  • No self-referrals
  • Violations may lead to suspension and loss of earnings

8. Identity & Payment Compliance

  • Valid ID (e.g. NIN) may be required before payout
  • Account name must match payment details
  • Spotlight may suspend accounts with inconsistencies

Privacy Policy (Partners)

1. Introduction

Spotlight Digital Services Ltd respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This policy applies only to partners using the platform.

2. Information We Collect

  • Full name, email address, and phone number
  • Referral activity and earnings data
  • Partner ID and account information
  • Government-issued ID (e.g. NIN) for verification
  • Bank account details for payouts

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Manage your partner account
  • Track referrals and calculate commissions
  • Process payouts
  • Prevent fraud and verify identity
  • Improve platform performance

4. Verification & Fraud Prevention

We may use your data to verify your identity, detect fraudulent activity, and enforce compliance with our terms.

5. Data Sharing

  • Financial institutions for partner payouts
  • Service providers supporting platform operations
  • Authorities when required by law

6. Data Security

We implement appropriate measures to protect your data from unauthorized access or misuse.

7. Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary to operate the partner program and comply with legal obligations.

8. Your Rights

  • Access your personal data
  • Request corrections
  • Request deletion (subject to limitations)

9. Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the platform means acceptance of updates.

10. Contact

Contact us through our official support channels for any privacy-related concerns.
