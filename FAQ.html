👉 Yes. Vendors are grouped into Verified and Unverified:

Verified Vendors:

These are Vendors whose business registration are confirmed from CAC and their proof of address from either a utility bill or lease agreement and bank statement. And also that have their ID verified. This category of Verified Vendors can obtain the Blue Badge for full verification. However, for those who verify only their ID and a profile picture including telephone and email via OTP during listing can obtain the Gray Bagde.

Unverified Vendors:

These are Vendors who may have done only the basic verification such as through OTP on their telephone and email and have neither verified their ID, address nor their business name. We strongly discourage any Vendor who operates as an Unverified Vendor. The minimum verification required is the ID verification to obtain the Gray Badge.
