Every new vendor on Spotlight gets a free 90-day trial — no credit card required, no hidden charges. Here's exactly what it includes and how it works:

What you get during the trial: Your listing is fully live and visible to customers from day one. You can add up to 3 products, 3 services, and 1 gallery image. You also get access to your Business Insight Dashboard to see who's finding you and how.

How long does it last? 90 days from the date you complete your registration and listing setup.

What happens after 90 days? Your listing automatically moves to the Free plan — it stays live and customers can still find you, but with reduced limits (1 product, 1 service). You can upgrade to a paid plan at any time before or after the trial ends to keep your full listing and unlock more features like additional products, more gallery images, branch management, and sponsored visibility.

Will I be charged automatically? No. Nothing is charged automatically. You only pay if and when you choose to upgrade — and even then, only after you confirm the plan you want.

After your 90-day trial, your listing automatically continues on the Free plan — you won't be charged anything unless you choose to upgrade. Paid plans give you more product/service listings, more gallery images, branch management, and sponsored visibility. Visit the Get Listed page to see full plan details and pricing.
