Our Story

The founders of Spotlight Digital Services Ltd had one guiding idea: every business deserves to be seen. Spotlight Directories grew out of that belief..

For too long, countless local businesses — salons, artisans, neighborhood shops, and home-based services — have been invisible online. Not because they lack quality, but because getting noticed digitally has been too complicated or too expensive. And the irony of the whole thing is that we need them at home, to make us look good, to organize functions, on the road, and even in our big offices to fix, repair or clean something. Good enough, they also need the products and services of the medium to larger firms.

We believe your SPOT is the life of your business — and when you’re not in your spot, you miss opportunities to thrive. That’s why we created Spotlight: a safe, reliable, and affordable way for small businesses to claim their spot on the web, on maps, and in the minds of customers — without needing a website or technical know-how. And for the big ones to also have variety in quality and convenience.

Inspired by the resilience and creativity of small businesses everywhere, our platform is designed to put you in control and help you grow with confidence. But growth isn’t just for the small players. Spotlight also brings value to larger businesses — helping searchers discover big sellers, understand their reach, compare prices, and enjoy the convenience of logistics networks and competitive market dynamics, all in one place.

And because we believe in giving back, Spotlight also connects communities to essential services such as govt hospitals, military & paramilitary, schools and more — making our platform not just a directory, but a tool for everyday life, for safety, security, directions, business visibility and growth.

Our story is about leveling the field: giving the smallest businesses a chance to shine, while helping customers connect with the bigger names effortlessly.

Because in the end, every business — big or small — deserves a spotlight.

Our Mission

To empower small and local businesses by providing easy, trusted, and flexible online listings — giving them visibility in our directory and beyond — so they can reach more customers, grow with confidence, and thrive in today’s digital world.

Our Vision

To become the go-to platform where local businesses shine — transforming how communities discover, connect with, and support the businesses that power everyday life.

What We Do

Smart Scheduling & Client Management

We also help in lead management with automatic miss calls follow up with appointment scheduling such that you never miss a call or a potential patronage. That way you get to reach out to such caller(s) for lead conversion at your earliest convenience while you give attention to other matters to grow your business.

Mobile Users & Walk-in Traffic

Listed businesses could attract mobile users nearby as they search and thereby increase walk-in traffic to their businesses. This leads to increase in potential new customers that come in overtime to boost turnover, profit and reduce dealing cost per unit.

Google Map Business Profile

We assist all listed Premium Vendors in creating and optimizing their Google Business Profile, making them visible on Google Search and Maps. Whether or not a Vendor has a physical office, this setup boosts online presence, builds customer trust, and increases reach. It highlights business details, services, photos, and reviews—helping Vendors attract more customers, gain credibility, and stand out in local searches.

Stand Out Locally

We are intentional in connecting ends - the SELLER and the BUYER. The Sellers are meant to list their businesses, with valid address(s) and contact details. On ther other hand, the Buyers are to simple tap on the search bar and type the category of what they want or a specific Vender (Seller) and Sellers in that category will display on your screen within a radius of 10km. If the search is via a specific Vendor's name, that particular Vendor or Seller details including all its branches within 10km will also display on your screen.

When you tap on a Vendor, the contact details and address will pop up for you to call. You can also invite such Vendor or place your order online and the purchased item is dispatched to you via a logistics network. Further, the Buyers can follow google map direction if he/she must be physically present to deal.
