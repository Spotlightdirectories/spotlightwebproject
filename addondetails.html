🤖 Smart Leads Mgt Auto-Respond & Scheduler

Never lose a lead again — even when you miss the call !

💡 How It Works

  1. Customer Calls You: Whether they tap “Call Vendor” in the Spotlight app or dial your number directly, the system detects the call.
  2. You Miss the Call: Spotlight automatically sends an SMS or WhatsApp message to your caller — acknowledging the missed call and promising a follow-up.
  3. Auto-Schedule Follow-Up: The system books a callback appointment based on your preset availability (e.g., 9–11 AM, 4–6 PM). You can reschedule anytime.
  4. Instant Vendor Notification: Receive caller details, missed call time, and scheduled callback slot instantly.

🚀 Why Vendors Love It

  • ✅ Never lose a sales lead again
  • ✅ Saves time with auto-booking
  • ✅ Keeps your business looking professional and responsive 24/7
  • ✅ Ideal for freelancers, artisans, or small teams without front desk staff
  • ✅ Keeps all call history organized in one place
  • ✅ Works even for calls made outside the app
  • ✅ Can increase your conversions up to 5×

⚙️ Vendor Setup

  1. Set your preferred callback hours in your vendor settings.
  2. Choose how to send auto-replies: SMS, WhatsApp, or both.
  3. Toggle “Auto-Respond ON” — ✅ Done — Spotlight handles the rest!

✔ Verified Badge — Build Trust with Customers

Verification helps customers quickly identify real, reachable, and trustworthy vendors on Spotlight.

💡 What Verification Means

  • Confirmed identity and/or business details
  • Reachable phone or WhatsApp number
  • Valid documents where applicable
  • Meets Spotlight’s basic trust standard

🏷 Verification Levels

Blue Badge

Full verification for registered businesses with ID, CAC documents, and proof of address.

Gray Badge

Partial verification for individual vendors using valid ID and profile photo.

No Badge

Basic phone and email verification during listing.

📄 Accepted Documents

  • ID: Driver’s License, International Passport, Smart NIN
  • Business: CAC Certificate, Status Report, MEMART
  • Address: Bank statement showing address

Verification is optional and free. It does not affect ranking or subscription plans, but verified vendors may receive more engagement due to increased trust.
